Six projects

1. No data skills, no return

I trained client teams across EMEA to turn design data into decisions and software into return.

Client training · design data · adoption

2. Seabed data guided a robot

I turned ROV survey points into a TIN seabed model sent directly to machine guidance. Team in Bangkok, me at sea.

Ormen Lange · 3D data · international delivery

3. I stumped AI. It got smarter.

I evaluated frontier-model outputs and turned expert STEM judgement into structured feedback.

Outlier · human-in-the-loop QA

4. Secure data access = income

I found paid content exposed without login. I rebuild its access control on Azure. The demo is in development.

Azure · Entra ID · access control

5. BIM. Drawings. Price. One day.

Using MCP prompts, I produced a Revit model, drawings and order-coded quantities.

Paid assignment · Revit · BIM

6. Paper hides. Data proves.

I moved site quality control from paper to mobile cloud checklists, with weekly standard reports.

Construction management · reporting · digital QA
